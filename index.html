Welcome

We're the Riverdale Math Team. We meet to work on fun problems together, learn tricks you won't see in class, and compete in math contests around New York City and beyond.

Anyone can join — no math experience needed. You don't have to be on an advanced track or have done a competition before. If you like puzzles (or want to find out whether you do), come by.

What we do

  • Work through practice problems and our own problem sets at meetings.
  • Take in-school contests like the AMC 10/12 and the Waterloo (CEMC) contests.
  • Compete as a team at local NYC tournaments (Stuyvesant, Horace Mann, Trinity).
  • Go on travel tournaments, including overnight trips — Princeton, Yale, and Johns Hopkins.

The full schedule is on the Competitions page.
