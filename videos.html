Fun Videos

Math videos we like — no homework attached. Good for a free period, a long bus ride to a tournament, or convincing a friend that math is cool. Have one to add? Tell a co-leader.

Infinity

  • How An Infinite Hotel Ran Out Of Room team favorite
    Veritasium

    Hilbert's Grand Hotel: a hotel with infinitely many rooms, all full, can always squeeze in one more guest — or infinitely many more. So how could it ever run out of space? A great first look at why some infinities are bigger than others.

  • How To Count Past Infinity
    Vsauce

    Picks up where the hotel leaves off: what comes after infinity? Ordinals, cardinals, and a tour of infinities that keep getting bigger.

  • 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + … = −1/12
    Numberphile

    The famous (and famously argued-about) claim that adding up all the positive integers gives a negative fraction. Watch it, then try to figure out where the "proof" bends the rules.

Competition problems

  • The hardest problem on the hardest test
    3Blue1Brown

    A probability question from the Putnam (the college version of the AMC) about random points on a sphere. The real lesson is the problem-solving strategy: solve a simpler version first.

  • The unexpectedly hard windmill question (2011 IMO, Q2)
    3Blue1Brown

    An olympiad problem that stumped some of the best young mathematicians in the world, even though it needs no advanced math — just the right way of looking at it.

Mind-bending

  • The most unexpected answer to a counting puzzle
    3Blue1Brown

    Two blocks slide and bounce off a wall. Count the collisions and you get the digits of π. Nobody expects that the first time.

  • Math's Fundamental Flaw
    Veritasium

    Gödel, the Game of Life, and the discovery that some true statements in math can never be proven. It makes a quick stop at Hilbert's hotel too.
